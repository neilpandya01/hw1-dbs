Hall High School · West Hartford, Connecticut

A public high school where every student finds their people.

Hall is one of West Hartford’s two public high schools: strong academics, nine state titles in ten years, and families who speak 37 languages at home. Whether you’re moving to town or starting ninth grade, we’ll help you find your way in.

What a day looks like

A day at Hall

School runs from 7:30 AM to 2:10 PM. Days alternate between odd and even, and each day has four long blocks of about 80 minutes: blocks 1, 3, 5 and 7 on odd days, 2, 4, 6 and 8 on even days.

Every day also has a 20-minute advisory. Lunch happens in the middle block, in one of three waves.

Questions families ask

Can my student start in the middle of the year?

Yes. Register with the district, then the counseling office will build a schedule and help your student catch up in each class.

How do buses work?

Bus routes are set by the district’s transportation office once you register. Transportation (opens in new tab) ↗

What about lunch?

Lunch is in the middle block, in one of three waves. Students can bring lunch or buy it; the daily menu is posted on this page.

What if English is new for my student?

English learner teachers work with students in class and one on one, and counselors make sure the schedule fits.

Can I read this site in another language?

Yes. Use Language at the top of every page to read the site in Spanish, Mandarin, Portuguese, Vietnamese, Arabic or many more languages.
