    01 · The Arts

    A Gala, a Choir Festival and a Day in Costume

    October is the busiest month of the fall for Hall’s musicians and actors. The Music & Fine Arts Department’s Gifts of Music Gala opens the season on Saturday, October 17. Five days later the Hall Players turn the whole school into a stage for Historical Figure Day, and the choirs close the month at Midfest.

    Every ensemble is open to new members at the start of a semester, and the theater program welcomes students to join backstage crews at any point in the year.
