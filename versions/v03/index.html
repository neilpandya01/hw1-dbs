    Our mission

    Hall High School prepares all students to be educated, responsible citizens who contribute collaboratively, independently, and innovatively to a dynamic global community.

    A comprehensive public high school of West Hartford Public Schools, #7 in the UConn Early College Experience program — 215 students earning 1,724 college credit hours.
